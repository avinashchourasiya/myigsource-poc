Welcome to MyIgSource

We are a community committed to helping each other and our loved ones manage a life with primary immunodeficiency (PI). Here is where you can find information on PI and living with Ig (immunoglobulin) treatment for PI.

Find peer-to-peer support from people living with PI. Connect with our Ig Community Support Team Advocates — people who live with or love someone with PI.

We invite you to join MyIgSource and become part of our community.
